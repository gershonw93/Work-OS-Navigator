Step-by-step guide

View plans and prepare a task pin

Open a drawing, inspect the detail, and place a task where the work happens.

4 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open a drawing and adjust the view2Choose Add pin, then tap the location3Write the task and review who receives it4Review pins from the drawing
1
Open a drawing and adjust the view
Open a plan by its name. Use minus and plus to change the zoom, Full screen for more room, or Open the file for the original drawing.
2
Choose Add pin, then tap the location
Add pin changes the control to Tap the plan. Tap the exact spot the task belongs to; position matters, so zoom in before placing it.
3
Write the task and review who receives it
Describe what needs to happen here. Choose the assignee, due date, and priority. Drop pin & create task is the saving action; Cancel backs out without creating the task.
4
Review pins from the drawing
Use All pins to review the drawing task list.
That's it!You've completed all 4 steps.
