Step-by-step guide

Use the Master Calendar

See schedules and due dates across jobs, then jump into the right project.

3 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Move through the calendar2Open an item to work on its job3Prepare a calendar subscription
1
Move through the calendar
Open Master Calendar for every project schedule and task due date in one place. Use Previous month, Next month or Today. Each item includes the job name; confirm that name before opening it.
2
Open an item to work on its job
Click a calendar item to jump into its project. Update dates and details in the job record, not by assuming the calendar changed them.
3
Prepare a calendar subscription
Connect to Calendar offers Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook, plus a link for another app. The dialog says schedules, task due dates and inspections appear as a read-only feed; it does not change SyteNav. Choose the destination calendar deliberately.
i
Keep the calendar feed link private: anyone who has it can see your schedule.
That's it!You've completed all 3 steps.
