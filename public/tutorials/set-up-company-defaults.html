Step-by-step guide

Set up company defaults

Choose the company details and starting defaults that fit your jobs.

2 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Add your company details2Choose how new jobs start
1
Add your company details
Open Settings > Company. Add the company name, contact details, address and license number. Choose your company type. A PNG or JPG logo up to 2MB can appear on downloaded daily logs, invoices and reports.
2
Choose how new jobs start
Default payment terms fill new quotes unless the quote has its own terms. Choose simple invoicing or progress billing (AIA), then the contract type that fits: cost-plus, fixed price or building to sell. Leave contract type unset if each job needs its own choice. Save Company when ready.
i
Each job can still differ from the company defaults.
That's it!You've completed all 2 steps.
