Step-by-step guide

Log a sub bill

Record a bill from a subcontractor or supplier. Logging the bill is separate from paying it.

5 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Bills from subs2Scan it or enter it yourself3Choose the sub and check the budget link4Enter what they are billing5Check the saved bill
1
Open Bills from subs
On an Active job, choose Finance > Bills from subs. Planning jobs hide this page until the job goes live.
2
Scan it or enter it yourself
Use Scan an invoice for a PDF, photo, or screenshot, or Create Invoice to enter the bill yourself. Review any scanned details before saving.
3
Choose the sub and check the budget link
Choose the subcontractor. Check which budget line the bill lands on. If the app warns there is no line, use Add a budget line for this contract before continuing.
4
Enter what they are billing
Choose Fixed amount, % of contract, or Scheduled item. Add the amount, description, and any charge breakdown, tax, or due date you need. Scroll down and click Create Invoice.
5
Check the saved bill
The bill appears under Pending Approval. Open it to review before moving it through approval and payment. Saving it has not paid the sub.
That's it!You've completed all 5 steps.
