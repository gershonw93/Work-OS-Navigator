Read the current plan and counters
Settings > Billing shows Your plan and Where you are up to. Planning, active and on-hold jobs count toward capacity; completed and cancelled jobs do not. Each house or unit counts as a job, but the development group itself does not.
2
Check live plan details
The Plans section is below the usage counters. Read the current prices, project limits and monthly AI scans there, - a screenshot in this guide is not a quote.
