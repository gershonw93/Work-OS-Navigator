Step-by-step guide

Add and manage a subcontractor

Keep the job scope, contract and schedule together without requiring the sub to have an account.

4 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Buyout > Subs & Team2Add a new sub or use your directory3Review scope, price and dates together4Open an existing sub to make changes
1
Open Buyout > Subs & Team
GC crew and subcontractors are separate. Choose Add Sub for a subcontractor, not Add Member. The subcontractor total shows contracted value, not what you have paid.
2
Add a new sub or use your directory
Enter a company and trade or choose From my directory. Contact details are optional. Attach a proposal or contract when you have one, and review any scanned values before saving.
3
Review scope, price and dates together
Use a short scope summary and optional item lines. A blank contract amount means Not set, not free. Add the payment milestones agreed with the sub and optional start/end dates; dates add the sub to the schedule. Save only when the record matches the agreement.
4
Open an existing sub to make changes
Expand its card, then choose Edit. Review the full agreement before saving changes. Remove is a different action and should not be used to correct a price or date.
That's it!You've completed all 4 steps.
