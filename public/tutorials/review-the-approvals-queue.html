Step-by-step guide

Review the approvals queue

Use the company-wide queue carefully, one job and one item at a time.

3 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Choose invoices or RFIs2Review before Approve or Reject3Answer RFIs on the job
1
Choose invoices or RFIs
Open Approvals. All combines the queue; Invoices and RFIs narrow it. Each row identifies the item, project, submitter, amount, status and date. Confirm these details against the job record before deciding.
2
Review before Approve or Reject
For an invoice, locate the correct project and supplier and review its supporting record first. The row offers Approve and Reject. These are decisions, not preview controls; do not click just to inspect them.
3
Answer RFIs on the job
Open the job's RFI section to read the question and its attachments before you answer.
i
Approving an invoice and paying it are separate steps.
That's it!You've completed all 3 steps.
