Step-by-step guide

Review project reports

Check the project, payments and compliance before preparing a PDF.

2 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Review Finance > Reports2Choose the report output
1
Review Finance > Reports
Wait for the report to load. Confirm the project identity, status and permit/inspection counts. Continue down the page for Payment Report and Compliance Report; pending invoices are not paid.
2
Choose the report output
Print / Save PDF is the whole-page output control. Print this section is available beside the Project Summary. Inspect the print preview and choose your destination before saving or printing.
i
A report is not proof of payment or coverage. Check invoice statuses and missing compliance documents on the job itself.
That's it!You've completed all 2 steps.
