Step-by-step guide

Create your first project

Set up your first job in SyteNav, from the project details to your dashboard. Use your own job details as you follow along.

8 stepsAbout 2 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Start a new project2Start typing - the address autofills3Owner / Client4Project type5Billing and payment6Size and dates7Create Project8Your project dashboard
1
Start a new project
Sign in to SyteNav, open Projects, and click New Project.
2
Start typing - the address autofills
Give your project a name. Start typing the street address: SyteNav brings up matching addresses. Choose one to autofill the address, then check the city, state, and ZIP.
3
Owner / Client
Choose your client from Owner / Client, or select + New client and enter their name.
4
Project type
There are three project types: Commercial, Mixed Use, and Residential. Choose the one that fits your job.
5
Billing and payment
Choose how you will bill: Simple invoicing or Progress billing (AIA). Then choose how the job pays you: Cost-plus, Fixed price, or Building to sell.
6
Size and dates
Set your Start Date. Add square footage, a target end date, or lot details if you need them. Those extra details are optional.
7
Create Project
Check your details, then click Create Project.
8
Your project dashboard
Your new job opens on Overview in Planning status. You are ready to start setting up the job.
That's it!You've completed all 8 steps.
