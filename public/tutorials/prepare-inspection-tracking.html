Step-by-step guide

Prepare inspection tracking

Separate required inspections from requests to the person who books them.

3 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Docs > Inspections2Build the required inspection checklist3Prepare the scheduling request and check who gets notified
1
Open Docs > Inspections
Required for this job is the checklist. Request Inspection is the scheduling request route. Show voided includes voided entries when you need to inspect them.
2
Build the required inspection checklist
Use Add in Required for this job. Tick the inspections the jurisdiction requires for this project. The app says adding to this list does not request it or tell anyone. Another type can be saved to the company list, so name it carefully.
3
Prepare the scheduling request and check who gets notified
Choose inspection type, date needed by and optional preferred time. Assign who schedules it, then check the notification summary and inspector/scheduling phone details. SyteNav does not contact the inspector: the person booking calls the jurisdiction, records the date given and later uploads the inspector card for the result.
That's it!You've completed all 3 steps.
