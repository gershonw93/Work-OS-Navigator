Step-by-step guide

Prepare a material receipt

Record the receipt against the right job and budget line.

4 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open the job Materials page2Take a photo, upload a file, or enter it manually3Link the job cost before saving4Check the extra choices and save deliberately
1
Open the job Materials page
Open Finance > Materials for this job and choose Add receipt. Check that you are on the right project before recording a cost.
2
Take a photo, upload a file, or enter it manually
Use the route that matches the receipt you have. Photo/upload can read the store and total; review those values against the receipt. Enter it manually opens the same accounting details without a scan.
3
Link the job cost before saving
Choose the correct budget line or create one. The form warns that an unlinked receipt is recorded but does not appear in Budget, project costs or Master Money. Review store, date, total, tax and category.
4
Check the extra choices and save deliberately
The budget picker offers existing lines and Create a new budget line. Before Save receipt, check whether to save the store in Directory and whether the customer already paid. These choices affect more than the receipt list.
i
Recording a receipt is not paying the supplier.
That's it!You've completed all 4 steps.
