Step-by-step guide

Reuse budget templates and jobs

Choose a saved template, a similar job or a budget sheet instead of starting every line again.

3 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Find reusable budgets2Choose what to bring into this job3Copy from a similar job or import a file
1
Find reusable budgets
Open Settings > Budget Templates. The list shows each template and line count. Expand one to review its categories, descriptions and budgeted amounts. Upload Excel is available for a new budget file.
2
Choose what to bring into this job
Open the project, then Finance > Budget > More budget actions > Use Template. Apply a saved template or choose a similar project. By default, line items come in with blank amounts; Also copy amounts brings the values too. Review the source carefully, especially when template names repeat.
3
Copy from a similar job or import a file
The project selector has search and a list of jobs. Choose the intended source, not just a familiar name. The same dialog offers Choose .xlsx / .csv for an estimate or budget sheet. More budget actions also offers Save as Template for reuse later.
That's it!You've completed all 3 steps.
