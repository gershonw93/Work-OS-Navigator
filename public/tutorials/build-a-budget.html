Step-by-step guide

Build a budget

Break the job into cost lines, then track what is budgeted, committed, and spent.

5 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open the job budget2Choose how to start3Add your cost lines4Include the costs around the trades5Check the budget as the job moves
1
Open the job budget
Open your project and choose Finance > Budget.
2
Choose how to start
Build from scratch with Add Line, use Use Template to copy a saved budget or similar job, or import an Excel/CSV estimate. Copying amounts is optional.
3
Add your cost lines
Choose a cost type and category, enter a description and Budgeted $, then click Add Line. Add a cost code or space if needed. Repeat for the job's other costs.
4
Include the costs around the trades
Use Add preconstruction costs for plans, permits, insurance, interest, contingency, and other soft costs. Select what applies and click Add selected, then fill in the amounts.
5
Check the budget as the job moves
Review Budgeted, Committed, Actual, and Variance by line. The summary shows your total budget and what is left to spend. Use each line's edit control to keep the plan current.
That's it!You've completed all 5 steps.
