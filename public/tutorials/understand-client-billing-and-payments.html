Step-by-step guide

Understand client billing and payments

Keep money requested from the client separate from money actually received.

3 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Finance > Billing the client2Prepare a deposit or one-off request3Record only a payment actually received
1
Open Finance > Billing the client
This page is money in from the client. Bills your subs sent you belong on Bills from subs. Review received funds, fee, paid-to-vendor and escrow balances together.
2
Prepare a deposit or one-off request
Payment stages from the estimate can appear here. Request a one-off amount lets you name the purpose and choose dollars or percent. Review the amount and request before creating or sending it.
3
Record only a payment actually received
Use Record Payment and enter its date, amount, method and reference. Decide whether unallocated money should remain client credit, whether it is a retainer/deposit, and whether it already exists in QuickBooks.
i
Approved sub costs and material receipts can feed client invoices, with your markup.
That's it!You've completed all 3 steps.
