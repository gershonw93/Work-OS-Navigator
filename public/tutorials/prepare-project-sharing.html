Step-by-step guide

Prepare project sharing

Choose an outside recipient or a team scope update deliberately.

3 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Choose the right sharing route2Prepare the outside-person update3Prepare a scope update for the right people
1
Choose the right sharing route
Docs > Sharing records what went out and who received it. Send to one person provides an outside person a link without an account. Send scope update tells the job team/trades through the bell and email. Documents are optional on both.
2
Prepare the outside-person update
Pick a contact or enter their name/email. Choose documents, title and message. Review whether they may send documents back and the link expiry. Confirm the recipient, contents and link access before Send update.
3
Prepare a scope update for the right people
Describe what changed and who needs to know. Attach a document if useful. If nobody on the job has an email address yet, add the crew or sub contact first, then review the recipients before sending.
i
Sharing can hand job documents to someone outside your company, so check exactly what is included.
That's it!You've completed all 3 steps.
