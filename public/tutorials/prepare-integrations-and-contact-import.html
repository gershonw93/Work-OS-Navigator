Step-by-step guide

Prepare integrations and contact import

Know what is shared before connecting another account.

2 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Review the connection scope2Keep imported contacts private until selected
1
Review the connection scope
Settings > Integrations shows QuickBooks Online and Google Contacts. The page describes a one-way push into QuickBooks for customers, vendors, bills and payments, client invoices and incoming money. Confirm the intended company and account before connecting.
2
Keep imported contacts private until selected
Set up Google Contacts opens Imported contacts. The screen says Google is read-only and the staging list is private to you. A contact becomes shared with the company only when added to the Directory. Connect the intended Google account, then review contacts before sharing them.
That's it!You've completed all 2 steps.
