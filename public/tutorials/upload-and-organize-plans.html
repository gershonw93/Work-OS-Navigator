Step-by-step guide

Upload and organize plans

Bring drawings into the job and keep the plan library easy to search.

5 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Field > Plans2Create a useful folder3Review the upload queue before adding4File the drawing in the right folder5Find drawings without losing their place
1
Open Field > Plans
Choose Upload Plans or drag drawings onto the tab. You can create folders first to organize the set.
2
Create a useful folder
Choose New Folder, enter a clear name, and create it. A folder is optional; root uploads can be moved later.
3
Review the upload queue before adding
Check each drawing name and type. The type is guessed from the filename, so correct any wrong guess before choosing Add plan. Remove files you did not intend to add.
4
File the drawing in the right folder
After the upload completes, use the drawing menu > Move to a folder. Choose the destination and Move. The same menu can open the original file; Delete plan removes the drawing.
5
Find drawings without losing their place
Open the folder to see its plans or use Search every plan on this job to look across the library. Open a drawing by its name. The scope-update control is for communication, not file organization.
That's it!You've completed all 5 steps.
