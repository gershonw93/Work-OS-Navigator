Overall Progress is calculated from task completion. Read the completed, in-progress, open, and overdue counts together; starting a task does not count it as finished.

Review progress by person or company
Use the lower section to see each owner and the tasks under them. Unassigned work appears separately. Update task owners and status in Field > Tasks rather than entering a percentage here.
