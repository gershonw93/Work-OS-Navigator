Step-by-step guide

Use the time clock

Check site setup, record a live punch, and review the weekly timesheet.

3 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Check the job location first2Start a punch from your camera-enabled device3Review the correct week before exporting
1
Check the job location first
Open Field > Time Clock. If the job has no map location, open the project gear and select the address from the suggestions or set its pin from the site. Until then, locations can be recorded but cannot be checked against the job.
2
Start a punch from your camera-enabled device
Choose Clock In when you are ready. The page requires a live selfie and records a timestamp and location. Allow the camera on the device you are using.
3
Review the correct week before exporting
Use the timesheet arrows to pick the week, check the logged hours and time entries, then Export when the record is ready.
That's it!You've completed all 3 steps.
