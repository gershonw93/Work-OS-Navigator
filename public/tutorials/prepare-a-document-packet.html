Step-by-step guide

Prepare a document packet

Group submission documents into a named packet, then review before sharing.

3 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Packets and start a group2Name the packet and choose its files3Keep creation separate from sending
1
Open Packets and start a group
In Files choose Packets, then New Packet. A packet is a group of files, for example the documents needed for a permit submission.
2
Name the packet and choose its files
Give the packet a clear name and describe its purpose. Add files with Upload new file; review the selected count and contents before Create Packet.
3
Keep creation separate from sending
The Shared tab tracks document sharing. Creating a packet is not proof that an expeditor received it. Review the audience and contents separately before sharing any real job package.
That's it!You've completed all 3 steps.
