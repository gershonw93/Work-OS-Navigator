Step-by-step guide

Group existing jobs in a development

Organize jobs you already have without importing new jobs.

2 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Name the group and find its jobs2Review moves before creating the group
1
Name the group and find its jobs
Projects > Bulk Add > Jobs you have. Enter the development name and use Find a job to narrow the list. Check the intended jobs by name and address before selecting them.
2
Review moves before creating the group
The checklist marks jobs already in a group with a move notice. Confirm that those jobs belong in the new development before using Create development. The screen says the jobs themselves stay the same and a development does not count toward active-project capacity.
That's it!You've completed all 2 steps.
