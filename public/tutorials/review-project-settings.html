Step-by-step guide

Review project settings

Check job details and billing choices before saving a change.

2 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Check the job and location2Choose the billing and contract pattern
1
Check the job and location
Open Project settings from the job header. Confirm the name, street, city, state and ZIP. The location panel says clock-ins are checked against this map pin; moving or clearing it changes a work-site reference. Check the selected customer, project type, status and dates too.
2
Choose the billing and contract pattern
The lower section separates Simple invoicing from Progress billing (AIA). Then choose Cost-plus, Fixed price or Building to sell to match the job. The app says this changes what Budget tracks: markup for cost-plus or a contract value for the other two. Optional area and lot details are below. Save Changes only after reviewing the whole form.
That's it!You've completed all 2 steps.
