Step-by-step guide

Build a project schedule

Add milestones, schedule vendors, and keep the job dates visible.

4 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Field > Schedule2Name the milestone and set its dates3Check the saved dates in the view you prefer4Edit dates and track what holds the work up
1
Open Field > Schedule
Awarded work feeds the schedule. Vendors without dates appear at the top: choose Set Dates when their site window is ready. For a job milestone, choose Add Milestone.
2
Name the milestone and set its dates
Give the item a clear label, choose start and end dates, and pick a color. Add the milestone when the window is correct.
3
Check the saved dates in the view you prefer
Calendar, Timeline, and List offer different ways to see the same job. List makes the item type, dates, and duration easy to check.
4
Edit dates and track what holds the work up
Open a schedule item to edit its dates, progress, and optional trade. The edit panel shows dependencies and why a date moved. Use Running late? when a delay needs attention, then review before saving changes.
That's it!You've completed all 4 steps.
