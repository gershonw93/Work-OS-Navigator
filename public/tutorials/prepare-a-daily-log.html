Step-by-step guide

Prepare a daily log

Record site conditions, work, delays, and observations in one field report.

4 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Field > Daily Logs2Check the date, weather, and crew3Explain delays and observations4Add photos and review before signoff
1
Open Field > Daily Logs
Choose New Log to start a report for the job.
2
Check the date, weather, and crew
Confirm the date first rather than assuming the default is your local workday. Use Auto-fill weather or choose the conditions and temperature yourself. Mark the subcontractors on site, write the general notes, and attach supporting files.
3
Explain delays and observations
Add a delay with its kind and what happened. Link a schedule line if available: recording the delay does not move dates until someone in the office acts on it. Include safety and quality observations, then answer the daily survey.
4
Add photos and review before signoff
Attach work photos and tag them to the right sub. Review the report, then the responsible site manager can draw or type their signoff and submit.
That's it!You've completed all 4 steps.
