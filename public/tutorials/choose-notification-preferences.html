Step-by-step guide

Choose notification preferences

Separate how you hear about events from who is told.

2 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Choose app and email channels2Choose who gets told
1
Choose app and email channels
Settings > Notifications lists each event with APP and EMAIL switches. In-app messages appear in the bell; email goes to the account address. Read each event before choosing its channels. Entries marked Coming soon are not ready workflows.
2
Choose who gets told
Settings > Who gets told controls the team members notified in the first place. Change opens the recipients for an event; each person still controls their own channels. Some events also always tell the person who created or owns the work.
That's it!You've completed all 2 steps.
