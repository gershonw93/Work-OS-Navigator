1
Filter before reviewing receipts
Open Materials for a company-wide receipt view. Search the store/category or choose a job. Receipts, Total Cost, Customer Paid and Owed by Customer summarize the current view. Confirm the job on each receipt before editing.
2
Notice receipts without a budget line
The warning says an unassigned receipt still counts in job costs but does not sit on a budget line. Use Assign it only after confirming the right job and budget line. Do not treat this warning as a missing receipt.
3
Start with a photo, file or manual entry
Add receipt offers Take photo, Upload file and manual entry. Check extracted store and total, then assign the right job and budget line before saving. See Prepare a material receipt for the detailed project form.
That's it!You've completed all 3 steps.
