Step-by-step guide

Prepare a bid request

Give bidders the same scope so the prices are useful to compare.

3 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Buyout > Quotes & Bids2Name the request and choose the package3Make the scope explicit
1
Open Buyout > Quotes & Bids
Choose New Request to prepare a package. Upload Quotes is a separate route for quotes you already have.
2
Name the request and choose the package
Enter a clear title and trade. Choose Labor + material, Labor only, Material only, or Measure & quote to match the actual arrangement. The material-supply responsibility changes with the package.
3
Make the scope explicit
List what is included, what is not included, and what you need back besides a price. For labor-only work, ask for the material list you need to buy. For material-only work, give suppliers the same item lines.
i
Before you create the request, check the due date, attachments and selected plans lower in the form, and the real recipients and final scope.
That's it!You've completed all 3 steps.
