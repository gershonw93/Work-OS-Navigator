Review role permissions

Understand role defaults and individual overrides before changing access.

1
Read the role access table
Settings > Permissions has Role Defaults. Choose the editing class, then read View, Create, Edit and Delete for each resource. Admin always has full access and cannot be edited. New class is available for a different access pattern.
2
Separate a role from an individual
Per-User Overrides lets you choose one teammate rather than changing the whole class. Confirm the person and the intended resources before altering access. View as offers a preview of what a role or teammate can see, with a banner and a way back.
