Open the history panel
Check the job name and address, then use Job history in the header. This is a record view, separate from the financial summary and today’s work.
2
Read action, actor and timing together
The panel groups entries by day and shows what happened, who is recorded and when. Scroll to older entries, then close the panel to return to the job.
i
History reports what was recorded. A bill-created entry is not proof of payment; open the bill itself for its current status.
