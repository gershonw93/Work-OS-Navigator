Step-by-step guide

Review subcontractor compliance

Check missing and expiring documents before work starts.

2 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Buyout > Compliance2Review the document details
1
Open Buyout > Compliance
Use the summary cards to spot missing, expiring and expired documents. Each subcontractor has a separate document list. Check the status, expiry and stored file rather than assuming a vendor is ready.
2
Review the document details
Use Upload on the needed document. For COI, review the status, expiry, insurer, policy number, coverage limits and additional-insured field against the actual document. Never mark Approved just to clear a warning.
i
The upload form also offers a PDF or image scan, a link to a stored file and notes; check what the scan read before you save. Request via email asks the vendor to upload it themselves. Not needed changes the requirement, so use it only when that document truly is not required.
That's it!You've completed all 2 steps.
