Step-by-step guide

Add and manage a customer

Keep the client, their projects, and your notes together.

3 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Create the customer2Open their project hub3Keep customer notes
1
Create the customer
Open Customers > New Customer. Enter the customer name and any contact name, email, or phone you have, then Create Customer.
2
Open their project hub
Use View customer to open the client. Check project counts and filter Planning, Active, or Completed. Use Add Project for a job under this customer.
3
Keep customer notes
Open Notes, enter the context you need to keep, and click Save Notes. Use Edit for the customer details and Documents for their files.
That's it!You've completed all 3 steps.
