Read Master Money

Compare job finances without confusing commitments, payments and customer cash.

1
Read the company roll-up
Master Money rolls budget, commitments and billing up across projects. Its cards show Client Received, Committed, Paid Out and Escrow Balance. The rows separate Received, Budgeted, Committed, Paid Out, Outstanding and Escrow for each job.
2
Open the job behind a number
Click the job row to open its financials. Check the job records before treating a roll-up as a bill to pay. Pending approval is not paid out; review the project invoice and payment history for the real state.
That's it!You've completed all 2 steps.
