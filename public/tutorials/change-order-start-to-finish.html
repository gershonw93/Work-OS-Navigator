Step-by-step guide

Change order start to finish

Record a scope or cost change, review it, and confirm the approved result.

6 stepsAbout 2 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Change Orders2Describe the change and its cost3Link the subcontract if it applies4Open the pending change5Review and make the decision6Confirm the approved result
1
Open Change Orders
On an Active job, choose Finance > Change Orders, then New Change Order. Planning jobs hide this page.
2
Describe the change and its cost
Enter a title, description, and amount. Use a negative amount for a deduction. Choose the reason and whether the GC or Sub requested it.
3
Link the subcontract if it applies
Choose the affected subcontract, or leave None. The form tells you that a linked, approved change adds the amount to that sub's contract. Click Create Change Order.
4
Open the pending change
Your change starts in Pending. Open the row and check the amount, reason, requester, subcontract, and description before deciding.
5
Review and make the decision
Add review notes if needed and use Save notes. Choose Approve or Reject after the change has been agreed through your normal approval process.
6
Confirm the approved result
Check that the change moved to Approved and the approved total updated. For a linked subcontract, look for Added to [sub]'s contract and check the contract in Buyout > Subs & Team.
i
This records the change and its review in SyteNav. It is not the client's signature.
That's it!You've completed all 6 steps.
