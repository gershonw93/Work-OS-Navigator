Use the job setup checklist

See what the job still needs without hunting through every tab.

2 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Setup on the correct job2Follow the missing setup items
1
Open Setup on the correct job
Check the project name and address, then use Setup in the header. The count shows completed items out of ten. The checklist follows an order because later steps depend on earlier ones.
2
Follow the missing setup items
Completed items show what is already in place, such as budget lines or plans. Missing items have links to the right work area. Add job people before assigning work; name subs before requesting their insurance and W-9s; share the client portal only once there is something worth seeing.
i
Hiding the checklist is a personal setting; Project Settings can show it again.
That's it!You've completed all 2 steps.
