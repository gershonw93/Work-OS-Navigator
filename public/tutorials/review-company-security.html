1
Protect important deletions
Settings > Security includes Delete protection for quotes, invoices, budget lines, quote requests and uploaded files. The panel offers a secret key and a require-key switch. The app says the key is stored hashed and can be replaced, not recovered. Share it only with the people allowed to delete.
2
Choose an inactivity limit
Auto sign-out applies to everyone in the company after the chosen period of inactivity, the next time each person loads the app. Review the interval and its own Save control, especially for shared computers.
That's it!You've completed all 2 steps.
