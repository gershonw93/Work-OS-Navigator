Step-by-step guide

Prepare a project permit

Keep permit details and the issuing authority together on the job.

3 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Docs > Permits2Upload for auto-fill or enter the permit details3Choose the actual permit type
1
Open Docs > Permits
Use Add Permit on the correct project. The list is where project permits and approvals are tracked.
2
Upload for auto-fill or enter the permit details
A photo/PDF upload can auto-fill fields. Review any extracted data against the actual permit. Or fill manually: type, number, description, status, issue/expiry dates and issuing authority. Add inspector contact details and notes before Add Permit.
3
Choose the actual permit type
Search the type picker or choose from building, electrical, demolition and the other available types. Do not substitute a nearby type or mark a permit issued before the authority has issued it.
That's it!You've completed all 3 steps.
