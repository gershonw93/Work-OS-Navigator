Read the financial summary

See contracted cost, invoice status and subcontract milestones without confusing pending with paid.

2 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Read the cards and payment bar by status2Expand a subcontract for milestones and history
1
Read the cards and payment bar by status
Open Finance > Financial Summary. Total Contracted is the subcontracts total; approved change orders have their own card. Outstanding includes approved and pending invoices. Total Paid is invoices marked paid, not merely entered or approved.
2
Expand a subcontract for milestones and history
Open the subcontract row to see payment milestones and invoice history. Generate Invoice is available beside a milestone; check existing invoice history first to avoid billing the same work twice.
That's it!You've completed all 2 steps.
