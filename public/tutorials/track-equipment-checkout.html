Step-by-step guide

Track equipment and check it out

Record your tools, where they are, and who has them.

4 stepsAbout 1 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Add the tool or machine2Record who is taking it and where3Check the live status4Flag equipment that needs work
1
Add the tool or machine
Open Equipment > Add equipment. Enter a name, then optional category, asset tag, serial number, and notes. Click Add.
2
Record who is taking it and where
Use Check out on an available item. Pick a teammate or type a crew name. Choose the job or yard, add a note if needed, then Confirm check-out.
3
Check the live status
The row shows the holder, location, and Checked out status. Use Check in when the equipment comes back and Show history to review its movement.
4
Flag equipment that needs work
Open the item's three-dot menu and choose Mark needs repair. Describe the problem so the team knows what needs fixing.
That's it!You've completed all 4 steps.
