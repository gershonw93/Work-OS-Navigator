Step-by-step guide

Invite your team and set roles

Bring your team into SyteNav and give each person the access they need. Start in Settings as an Admin.

6 stepsAbout 2 min Updated October 11, 2026
Steps
1Open Team & Users2Add your teammate3Choose the right role4Review and send5Change a teammate's role6Check access when you need to
1
Open Team & Users
Open Settings > Team & Users, then click + Invite User.
2
Add your teammate
Enter their Full Name and Email Address. Use the email they will sign in with.
3
Choose the right role
Choose a role that fits their work. Admin, Office Staff, and Project Manager are the main office roles. Field roles are available too.
i
Roles at a glance Admin: Full access, including users and settings. Office Staff: View projects, submit invoices, and manage compliance documents. Project Manager (PM): Create and edit projects, tasks, RFIs, and daily logs. Choose the access that person needs. Role defaults and individual overrides can change what someone can do.
4
Review and send
Check the name, email, and role, then click Send Invite when you are ready.
5
Change a teammate's role
Back in Team & Users, open the role beside a teammate and choose their new role. Admin and Owner rows may not offer this control.
6
Check access when you need to
Open Permissions to review Role Defaults or Per-User Overrides. Use View as to preview what a role or teammate can see. Admin always has full access.
That's it!You've completed all 6 steps.
